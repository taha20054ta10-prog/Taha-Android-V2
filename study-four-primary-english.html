1. Daily routine

We use the present simple for habits. The third person singular often adds -s or -es. Read the complete sentence to find time words.

💡 مثال مشروح

I study every day. She studies at night.
❓ سؤال للتأكد من الفهم: When does she study?

✅ الإجابة: At night.

2. Past simple

💡 مثال مشروح

❓ سؤال للتأكد من الفهم: When did I visit my friend?

✅ الإجابة: Yesterday.

3. Future plans

We often use will for predictions or decisions and going to for plans. Choose the form that fits the context of the conversation.

💡 مثال مشروح

I am going to revise my lessons tonight.
❓ سؤال للتأكد من الفهم: What am I going to do?

✅ الإجابة: Revise my lessons.

6. Present continuous

Use am, is or are with an -ing verb to describe an activity happening now, and check subject–verb agreement.

💡 مثال مشروح

They are playing in the garden now.
❓ سؤال للتأكد من الفهم: What are they doing?

✅ الإجابة: They are playing.

7. Comparatives

For comparisons between two things, adjectives may take -er or more. We usually add than after the comparative.

💡 مثال مشروح

This street is wider than that street.
❓ سؤال للتأكد من الفهم: Which street is wider?

✅ الإجابة: This street.

8. Paragraph writing

Begin with a topic sentence and support it with specific examples. End with a closing sentence and check spelling and punctuation.

💡 مثال مشروح

My city is beautiful. It has parks. I enjoy living here.
❓ سؤال للتأكد من الفهم: What is the paragraph about?

✅ الإجابة: My city.
